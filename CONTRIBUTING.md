# Editorial and Attribution Guidelines

This is a public, personally maintained teaching-preparation repository.

## For every lesson

1. Identify the **specific speaker, talk title, conference, and teaching date**.
2. Link to the **official English address** and its named sections.
3. Cite every assigned scripture using a direct Church scripture link.
4. Distinguish **speaker quotation**, **paraphrase**, **scripture**, and **facilitator-created ideas**.
5. Do not copy whole conference talks into the repository; use direct links instead.
6. Include a minute-by-minute discussion plan and a short application invitation.
7. Avoid personal data, confidential ministering details, or members' identifying experiences.
8. Do not imply that these are official Church materials.

## Suggested commit examples

- `Prepare Uchtdorf unity discussion for Oct 18`
- `Revise timing based on lesson feedback`
- `Add April 2027 General Conference discussion`
