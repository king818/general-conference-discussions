### Consider

1. **Our covenants** — What does [Mosiah 18:8–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/18?id=p8-p9&lang=eng) teach about carrying burdens together?
2. **Our differences** — How does [Ephesians 2:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/eph/2?id=p14&lang=eng) help us understand the Savior's ability to overcome division?
3. **Our example** — Why did the people in [4 Nephi 1:15–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/4-ne/1?id=p15-p17&lang=eng) become one?
4. **Our worship** — How does [Doctrine and Covenants 20:77](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/20?id=p77&lang=eng) help us remember our relationship with Christ and fellow disciples?
