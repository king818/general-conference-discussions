# Using the Language of Peacemakers

## Elders Quorum Discussion Handout

**Sunday, October 25, 2026**  
**October 2026 General Conference**  
**Elder Alfred Kyungu — Of the Seventy**

[Read the Official General Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/47kyungu?lang=eng)

### Our Central Question

> **How can we become more like Jesus Christ by changing the words we choose, especially when conversations become difficult?**

### The Message

**Speaker's exact words:**

> "We must choose language that strengthens confidence and preserves relationships."

— Elder Alfred Kyungu

Our words can build trust, express love, strengthen faith, and encourage reconciliation. They can also cause unnecessary pain and contention.

Choosing the language of peacemakers is an important part of becoming a disciple of Jesus Christ.

---

## 1. Our Words Should Edify

**Ephesians 4:29**

> "Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers."

[Read Ephesians 4:29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/eph/4?lang=eng&id=p29#p29)

**Matthew 12:34**

> "For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh."

[Read Matthew 12:34–37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/12?lang=eng&id=p34-p37#p34)

**Discuss:**

How can we speak truthfully while still helping others feel respected, valued, and loved?

**Key Principle:** Christlike communication begins with a Christlike heart.

---

## 2. Bury the Language of Contention

**Alma 24:19**

The converted Anti-Nephi-Lehies buried their weapons of war as an expression of their commitment to peace.

[Read Alma 24:19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/24?lang=eng&id=p19#p19)

**Speaker's exact words:**

> "we too can bury the language of contention for peace."

— Elder Alfred Kyungu

**3 Nephi 11:29–30**

Jesus Christ teaches that contention is not of Him and that we should do away with it.

[Read 3 Nephi 11:29–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p29-p30#p29)

**Discuss:**

What expressions or communication habits might we need to bury as we become more like Jesus Christ?

**Key Principle:** Conversion includes deliberately abandoning habits that damage relationships.

---

## 3. Choose Words That Bring Peace

**Colossians 4:6**

> "Let your speech be alway with grace"

[Read Colossians 4:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/col/4?lang=eng&id=p6#p6)

### Practical Examples

| Instead of saying... | Consider saying... |
|---|---|
| "You never listen." | "Can we take time to understand each other?" |
| "This is all your fault." | "How can we work through this together?" |
| "You're wrong." | "I understand your perspective, but I see this differently." |
| "Just stop talking." | "Can we take a break and continue when we're calmer?" |
| "You always mess things up." | "Let's find a better way forward." |

*These are lesson-created examples illustrating Elder Kyungu's teachings, not direct quotations from his talk.*

**Discuss:**

How might our relationships change if we consistently replaced accusations with respect, understanding, and kindness?

**Key Principle:** We can speak firmly and honestly without speaking harshly.

---

## 4. Jesus Christ Is the Prince of Peace

**John 14:27**

> "Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you"

[Read John 14:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?lang=eng&id=p27#p27)

Jesus Christ offers us peace and invites us to become instruments of His peace.

Through faith in Him, repentance, prayer, and daily practice, our hearts and words can change.

**Key Principle:** The Savior's peace can influence how we respond even when we face disagreement or difficulty.

**STEP 2 — REPLACE**

____________________________________

**STEP 3 — PRACTICE**

Use your new response in at least one real conversation this week.

**STEP 4 — REFLECT**

At the end of each day, consider:

- Did my words build up or tear down?
- Did I listen before responding?
- Did I speak truthfully and respectfully?
