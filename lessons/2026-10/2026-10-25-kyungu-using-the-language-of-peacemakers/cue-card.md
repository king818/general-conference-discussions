# ELDERS QUORUM — FACILITATOR CUE CARD

**Sunday, October 25, 2026 | 30 Minutes**

**Talk:** *Using the Language of Peacemakers*  
**Speaker:** Elder Alfred Kyungu, Of the Seventy  
**Conference:** October 2026

[Official Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/47kyungu?lang=eng)

**CENTRAL QUESTION**

> How can we become more like Jesus Christ by changing the words we choose, especially when conversations become difficult?

---

## 0:00–4:00 | OPENING — WORDS MATTER

**SAY:** "Our words can either strengthen relationships or damage them. Peacemaking is part of following Jesus Christ."

**SPEAKER QUOTE:**

> "We must choose language that strengthens confidence and preserves relationships."

**ASK:** Why do words have such a lasting influence on relationships?

**SOURCE:** [Kyungu, October 2026](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/47kyungu?lang=eng)

**TRANSITION:** "Why does the Savior care so much about the words we speak?"

---

## 4:00–12:00 | MAIN DOCTRINE — SPEAK TO EDIFY

**READ:** [Ephesians 4:29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/eph/4?lang=eng&id=p29#p29)

**KEY PHRASE — SCRIPTURE:** "that it may minister grace unto the hearers."

**ASK:**
- What does it mean for words to minister grace?
- Can we disagree and still edify?
- What does our speech reveal about our hearts?

**CONNECT:** [Matthew 12:34–37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/12?lang=eng&id=p34-p37#p34)

**DOCTRINE:** A Christlike heart leads to Christlike speech.

**TRANSITION:** "How can we deliberately abandon words that create contention?"

---

## 12:00–20:00 | BOOK OF MORMON — BURY THE WEAPONS

**READ:** [Alma 24:19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/24?lang=eng&id=p19#p19)

**SPEAKER QUOTE:**

> "we too can bury the language of contention for peace."

**ASK:**
- What verbal weapons do people sometimes use?
- Why are harmful communication habits difficult to abandon?
- How can conversion to Christ help us change?

**CONNECT:** [3 Nephi 11:29–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p29-p30#p29)

**DOCTRINE:** Peacemaking requires repentance and deliberate choices.

**TRANSITION:** "What would this look like in a real conversation tomorrow?"

---

## 20:00–27:00 | APPLICATION — REPLACE THE WORDS

**ACTIVITY:** Invite members to rephrase one or two statements.

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---|---|
| "You never listen." | "Can we talk and understand each other?" |
| "This is all your fault." | "How can we work through this?" |
| "You're wrong." | "I see it differently. May I explain?" |

*These are facilitator-created examples, not speaker quotations.*

**ASK:** What changes when we replace accusation with understanding?

**CONNECT:** [Colossians 4:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/col/4?lang=eng&id=p6#p6)

**WEEKLY CHALLENGE:**

**Bury One Phrase. Plant One Phrase.**

1. Identify one harmful phrase or habit.
2. Choose a peacemaking replacement.
3. Practice it this week.
4. Reflect and begin again as needed.

**TRANSITION:** "The power to change our hearts and words comes through Jesus Christ."

---

## 27:00–30:00 | CLOSING — CHRIST'S PEACE

**READ:** [John 14:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?lang=eng&id=p27#p27)

**ASK (silent reflection):** What one phrase or habit will I change this week?

**SAY:** "Peacemaking begins in our hearts, continues through our choices, and becomes visible in our words."

**INVITE:** Bury one contentious phrase. Practice one Christlike replacement.

**TESTIMONY PROMPT:** Bear personal testimony of Jesus Christ as the Prince of Peace, His power to change hearts, and the blessings of speaking with love.

---

**TIME CHECK:** 4 → 12 → 20 → 27 → 30 minutes

**IF SHORT ON TIME:** Ephesians 4:29 → Alma 24:19 → One replacement → John 14:27.

**REMEMBER:** Invite, don't lecture. Protect privacy. Welcome different perspectives. Keep Christ at the center.

**FINAL MESSAGE:** Our words can become instruments of the Savior's peace.
