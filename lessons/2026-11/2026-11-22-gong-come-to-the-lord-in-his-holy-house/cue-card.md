# ELDERS QUORUM — FACILITATOR CUE CARD

**Sunday, November 22, 2026 | 30 Minutes**

**Talk:** *Come to the Lord in His Holy House*  
**Speaker:** Elder Gerrit W. Gong  
**Conference:** October 2026 General Conference

[Official Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/12gong?lang=eng)

**CENTRAL QUESTION**

> How can we make the house of the Lord a greater source of spiritual safety, healing, and covenant belonging in our lives?

**MAIN MESSAGE:** The temple brings us closer to Jesus Christ, strengthens our covenant relationships, and invites us to experience spiritual peace now.

---

## 0–4 min | Open — The Lord Invites Us Home

**ASK:** What commotion do we face today?

**TRANSITION:** "Why does the temple offer such powerful blessings? Elder Gong gives us three foundations."

---

## 4–14 min | Doctrine — Three doctrinal foundations

**READ:** [Moses 1:39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/1?id=p39&lang=eng); [Doctrine and Covenants 132:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/132?id=p7&lang=eng)

**SAY:** The three doctrinal foundations:

1. Eternal families — Heavenly Father's work is to bring His children home. Moses 1:39
2. Moral agency — God honors choice; Christ offers repentance, forgiveness, and healing. Alma 34:32–33
3. Holy Spirit of Promise — Eternal covenant blessings are confirmed as covenants are faithfully honored. D&C 132:7

**ASK:** How do covenants, agency, and faithfulness shape eternal relationships?

**TRANSITION:** "Because Christ makes repentance possible, we do not have to wait for a perfect moment."

---

## 14–20 min | Scripture — Do Not Delay Spiritual Peace

**READ:** [Alma 34:32–33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/34?id=p32-p33&lang=eng)

**ASK:** What spiritual blessing are we postponing?

**TRANSITION:** "Now let's make these teachings practical through five elements of temple culture."

---

## 20–27 min | Application — Five temple culture practices

**SAY:** The five elements of personal temple culture:

1. Treasure spiritual blessings, not only beautiful buildings.
2. Come more often, bring family names, and serve where possible.
3. Focus on Jesus Christ in every temple experience.
4. Practice gratitude for every temple, ordinance, and covenant.
5. Prepare personally, including ceremonial temple clothing where possible.

**ASK:** Which practice could strengthen our lives this week?

**TRANSITION:** "Choose one faithful action. The goal is to draw closer to Jesus Christ."

---

## 27–30 min | Close — Invitation and testimony

**INVITE:** This week, choose ONE:

- Attend or schedule a temple visit.
- Prepare a family name.
- Study Moses 1:39 and Alma 34:32–33.
- Express gratitude for temple blessings.
- Strengthen one covenant-related habit.

**SAY:** "Jesus Christ is the center of temple worship. Through Him, we find forgiveness, healing, peace, and hope for eternal families."

Bear brief testimony of Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.
