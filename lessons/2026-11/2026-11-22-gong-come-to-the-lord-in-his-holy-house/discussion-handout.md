# Come to the Lord in His Holy House

## Elders Quorum Discussion Handout

**Sunday, November 22, 2026**  
**October 2026 General Conference**  
**Elder Gerrit W. Gong**

[Read the Full Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/12gong?lang=eng)

### Our central question

> **How can we make the house of the Lord a greater source of spiritual safety, healing, and covenant belonging in our lives?**

### Key scriptures

1. **God's eternal purpose** — [Moses 1:39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/1?id=p39&lang=eng) — God's work and glory center on the immortality and eternal life of His children. *Reflect: How does knowing God's purpose change the way I view my family and my future?*
2. **The time to prepare is now** — [Alma 34:32–33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/34?id=p32-p33&lang=eng) — This life is the time to prepare to meet God. Repentance and spiritual growth should not be needlessly delayed. *Reflect: What can I begin today rather than postpone?*
3. **Eternal covenants** — [Doctrine and Covenants 132:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/132?id=p7&lang=eng) — The Lord establishes the divine authority by which sacred covenants and ordinances can have eternal effect. *Reflect: How can I honor my covenants more intentionally?*

### Three truths about temple blessings

| Foundation | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| 1. Eternal families | Heavenly Father desires His children to return to Him, united and happy forever. |
| 2. Moral agency | God respects our choices. Jesus Christ makes repentance, change, forgiveness, and healing possible. |
| 3. Holy Spirit of Promise | The Holy Ghost confirms sacred ordinances and covenants as we faithfully honor them. |

*These statements paraphrase Elder Gong's three doctrinal foundations.*

### Five ways to build a personal temple culture

**Personal reflection:** Which of these practices can I strengthen?

1. Treasure the spiritual blessings of the temple.
2. Attend more often, prepare family names, or serve where possible.
3. Recognize Jesus Christ as the center of temple worship.
4. Speak of the temple with love and gratitude.
5. Prepare reverently, including personal ceremonial temple clothing where possible.

### A thought to carry home

*Facilitator-created reflection:*

> Elder Gong teaches that the house of the Lord is a place where we can receive divine blessings, deepen our relationship with Jesus Christ, and prepare to return to Heavenly Father.
>
> Discuss or ponder: What would change in my life if I approached temple worship not merely as an appointment, but as a continuing relationship with the Lord?
