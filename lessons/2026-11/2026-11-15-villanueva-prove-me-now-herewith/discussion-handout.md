# Prove Me Now Herewith

## Elders Quorum Discussion Handout

**Sunday, November 15, 2026**  
**October 2026 General Conference**  
**Elder Moisés Villanueva**

[Read the Full Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/53villanueva?lang=eng)

**Theme:** Trusting God's promises and recognizing His blessings

### Our central question

> **How can I trust the Lord's promises when His blessings do not come in the way or at the time I expect?**

### Key scriptures

1. **The windows of heaven** — [Malachi 3:10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/mal/3?id=p10&lang=eng) — The Lord invites His people to live the law of tithing and trust His promise to bless them. *Reflect: What does it mean to trust the Lord without deciding in advance what His blessings must look like?*
2. **Obedience and blessings** — [Doctrine and Covenants 130:20–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/130?id=p20-p21&lang=eng) — God's blessings are connected to divine laws. *Reflect: How does obedience express our trust in Heavenly Father?*
3. **The widow of Zarephath** — [1 Kings 17:8–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-kgs/17?id=p8-p16&lang=eng) — The widow acted in faith during severe scarcity, and the Lord fulfilled His promise. *Reflect: When have you needed to act in faith before seeing the outcome?*

### Three key takeaways

1. God's promises are trustworthy. We can trust His goodness without assuming every blessing will arrive on our preferred timetable.
2. Faithful obedience can require sacrifice. Elder Villanueva's mother and the widow of Zarephath both illustrate trusting God during material hardship.
3. Some blessings are subtle. Gratitude, spiritual guidance, opportunities to learn, and strength through adversity may be easier to overlook than dramatic answers.
