# ELDERS QUORUM — FACILITATOR CUE CARD

**Sunday, November 15, 2026 | 30 Minutes**

**Talk:** *Prove Me Now Herewith*  
**Speaker:** Elder Moisés Villanueva  
**Conference:** October 2026 General Conference

[Official Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/53villanueva?lang=eng)

**CENTRAL QUESTION**

> How do we trust the Lord when His blessings come differently than expected?

---

## 0–4 min | Open — Expectations

**SAY:** God's blessings do not always come in the form or timing we expect.

**ASK:** When has an unexpected answer taught you something about trust?

**TRANSITION:** "Let's read the promise behind the talk's title."

---

## 4–12 min | Doctrine — Windows of heaven

**READ:** [Malachi 3:10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/mal/3?id=p10&lang=eng); [Doctrine and Covenants 130:20–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/130?id=p20-p21&lang=eng)

**SAY:** Villanueva's mother supported eight children and chose to pay a full tithe.

**ASK:** What does "prove me now herewith" teach us? Why obey without a guaranteed timetable?

**TRANSITION:** "Let's look at another person who acted before seeing the blessing."

---

## 12–20 min | Scripture — Widow of Zarephath

**READ:** [1 Kings 17:12–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-kgs/17?id=p12-p16&lang=eng)

**ASK:** What might the widow have felt? When have you acted in faith without knowing the outcome?

**TRANSITION:** "How can we notice blessings that are less dramatic?"

---

## 20–27 min | Application — Subtle blessings

**SAY:** Villanueva wrote down blessings he had previously overlooked.

**ASK:** What quiet blessing have you noticed? How does gratitude strengthen faith?

**INVITE:** List three blessings. Review one faithful next step. Express gratitude.

---

## 27–30 min | Close — Testimony

**ASK:** What will you carry into this week?

**SAY:** "Trust the Lord. Recognize His blessings. Continue in faith."

Bear brief testimony of Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.

Facilitator-created prompts and transitions. Stories are paraphrased from Elder Villanueva's official address.

## Facilitation guardrails

**Time discipline:** One opening response; two or three responses per main discussion; protect the final three minutes.

**Pastoral discipline:** No pressure to disclose finances. No prosperity-gospel interpretation. Welcome experiences of waiting, uncertainty, and unanswered prayer.
