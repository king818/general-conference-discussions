# ELDERS QUORUM — FACILITATOR CUE CARD

**Sunday, November 1, 2026 | 30 Minutes**

**Talk:** *Love Like Jesus*  
**Speaker:** Elder Ronald A. Rasband, Quorum of the Twelve Apostles  
**Conference:** October 2026 — Saturday Afternoon Session

[Official Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/210rasband?lang=eng)

**CENTRAL QUESTION**

> How can we love someone as Jesus Christ does when we cannot change that person's choices?

---

## 0–3 min | Opening

**SAY:** We cannot control others' choices, but we can choose how to love.

**ASK:** What is the difference between love and control?

---

## 3–10 min | Love and agency

**READ:** [Matthew 22:37–39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/22?lang=eng)

**ASK:** How do we love without approving every choice?

---

## 10–18 min | Alma and prayer

**READ:** [Mosiah 27:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/27?lang=eng); [Alma 36:17–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/36?lang=eng)

**ASK:** What can prayer accomplish that pressure cannot?

---

## 18–24 min | Prodigal son

**READ:** [Luke 15:17–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/15?lang=eng)

**ASK:** What made the father's love unmistakable?

---

## 24–28 min | Application

**READ:** [Matthew 11:28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/11?lang=eng)

**ASK:** Which of Elder Rasband's three suggestions will you apply?

---

## 28–30 min | Closing

**CLOSING:** Recap: Love, pray, trust Christ. Invite one act of love. Bear testimony.

---

## Three exact speaker quotes

**SPEAKER QUOTE 1:** "We cannot change their hearts, but He can."

**SPEAKER QUOTE 2:** "Love is expressed in prayer."

**SPEAKER QUOTE 3:** "Trust Him. He's got this!"

**SOURCE:** Elder Rasband, *Love Like Jesus*.

---

## Transition lines

- **Opening → Doctrine:** "Let's begin with what Jesus called the greatest commandments."
- **Doctrine → Alma:** "How do we continue loving when we cannot change someone's choices?"
- **Alma → Prodigal son:** "Prayer teaches us to trust God; the prodigal son's father teaches us how to welcome."
- **Scripture → Application:** "What can we actually do differently this week?"
- **Application → Testimony:** "Our hope rests not in our ability to control outcomes, but in Jesus Christ."

---

## Final invitation

**SAY:** "This week, choose one person. Express Christlike love in one specific way, without demanding a particular response. Pray for that person and for your own heart."

**Time discipline:** Aim for three responses per major question. At minute 24, move to application regardless of unfinished discussion. Protect the final two minutes.
