# Love Like Jesus

## Elders Quorum Discussion Handout

**Sunday, November 1, 2026**  
**October 2026 General Conference — Saturday Afternoon Session**  
**Elder Ronald A. Rasband — Quorum of the Twelve Apostles**

[Read the Full Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/210rasband?lang=eng)

### Our central question

> **How can we love someone as Jesus Christ does when we cannot change that person's choices?**

### Key scriptures

1. Love is the greatest commandment. — [Matthew 22:36–40](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/22?lang=eng) — How can we show love while remaining faithful to God's commandments?
2. Faithful prayer invites God's help. — [Mosiah 27:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/27?lang=eng) · [Alma 36:17–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/36?lang=eng) — What do Alma the Elder and Alma the Younger teach about prayer, agency, and the Savior's power?
3. Christlike love welcomes repentance. — [Luke 15:17–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/15?lang=eng) — What can we learn from the father's compassion toward his returning son?
4. Jesus Christ offers rest. — [Matthew 11:28–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/11?lang=eng) — What burden could we entrust more fully to the Savior?

### Three teachings from Elder Rasband

**TALK PARAPHRASE**

1. Let go of guilt. Do not allow despair over another person's choices to extinguish love and hope.
2. Put people before problems. A person's worth is greater than any problem we want to resolve.
3. Remember Jesus Christ. Turn to Him through scripture study, prayer, and trust in His redeeming power.

**SPEAKER QUOTE**

> "We cannot change their hearts, but He can."

— Elder Ronald A. Rasband, *Love Like Jesus*

### This week's invitation

**Choose one act of Christlike love.**

- Listen with patience instead of correcting.
- Express appreciation or offer appropriate help.
- Pray sincerely for someone and for a more Christlike heart.
- Take a safe, respectful step toward reconciliation.
