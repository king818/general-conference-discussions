GREATER FAITH AND HOPE IN CHRIST

1. When we cannot fix things

Elder Eyring describes a father learning that his son had unexpectedly died. His experience reminds us that faithful disciples sometimes face circumstances beyond human control.

Read: Alma 7:11–12; Psalm 55:22.

Discuss: How is relying on the Savior different from giving up personal responsibility?

2. The Savior promised a Comforter

Before His Crucifixion, Jesus promised His disciples the Holy Ghost, who would abide with them.

Read: John 14:16–18.

Discuss: How have you recognized the Holy Ghost's comfort through prayer, scripture, a priesthood blessing, or another person's kindness?

3. Peace does not require perfect circumstances

Elder Eyring recounts giving a priesthood blessing to a father grieving the death of his unborn child. The promised blessing was peace, not the reversal of the loss.

Read: Philippians 4:6–7.

Discuss: Can a person experience grief and divine peace at the same time? What helps us recognize that peace?

4. The Resurrection gives us eternal hope

Jesus Christ overcame physical death through His Resurrection. Through His Atonement, He also made it possible for us to overcome spiritual separation from God.

Read: 2 Nephi 9:10–12; 1 Corinthians 15:20–22.

Discuss: How does the reality of resurrection change our perspective on loss, illness, and mortality?

5. We are called to comfort one another

Our baptismal covenant includes sharing one another's burdens, mourning together, and offering comfort.

Read: Mosiah 18:8–10.

Discuss: What is more helpful to someone who is suffering: an explanation, advice, patient listening, or practical service? How do we discern what is needed?

6. Seek the Holy Ghost's constant companionship

The Holy Ghost can guide us toward Jesus Christ as we pray, repent, keep our covenants, worship, and respond to spiritual impressions.

Read: Doctrine and Covenants 88:63; Moroni 10:5.

Discuss: What one daily habit would help you become more receptive to the Holy Ghost?
