# Comfort from the Holy Ghost

## Elders Quorum Discussion Handout

**Sunday, November 8, 2026**  
**October 2026 General Conference**  
**Elder Matthew J. Eyring**

[Read the Full Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/29eyring?lang=eng)

### Our central question

> **How can we receive the Savior's peace when life presents burdens we cannot fix?**

### The lesson at a glance

- **Jesus Christ** — our Savior, Redeemer, and source of eternal hope
- **The Holy Ghost** — communicates truth, peace, guidance, and comfort
- **Receive comfort** — pray, trust, repent, listen, endure
- **Give comfort** — mourn, listen, serve, minister
- **The result** — greater faith and hope in Christ

### Key scriptures

1. The Savior knows how we feel. — [Alma 7:11–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/7?id=p11-p12&lang=eng) · [Psalm 55:22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ps/55?id=p22&lang=eng) — How is relying on the Savior different from giving up personal responsibility?
2. The Savior promised a Comforter. — [John 14:16–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?id=p16-p18&lang=eng) — How have you recognized the Holy Ghost's comfort through prayer, scripture, a priesthood blessing, or another person's kindness?
3. Peace does not require perfect circumstances. — [Philippians 4:6–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/phil/4?id=p6-p7&lang=eng) — Can a person experience grief and divine peace at the same time? What helps us recognize that peace?
4. The Resurrection gives us eternal hope. — [2 Nephi 9:10–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/9?id=p10-p12&lang=eng) · [1 Corinthians 15:20–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/15?id=p20-p22&lang=eng) — How does the reality of resurrection change our perspective on loss, illness, and mortality?
5. We are called to comfort one another. — [Mosiah 18:8–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/18?id=p8-p10&lang=eng) — What is more helpful to someone who is suffering: an explanation, advice, patient listening, or practical service? How do we discern what is needed?
6. Seek the Holy Ghost's constant companionship. — [Doctrine and Covenants 88:63](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/88?id=p63&lang=eng) · [Moroni 10:5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/moro/10?id=p5&lang=eng) — What one daily habit would help you become more receptive to the Holy Ghost?

### Teachings from the discussion

1. **When we cannot fix things** — Elder Eyring describes a father learning that his son had unexpectedly died. His experience reminds us that faithful disciples sometimes face circumstances beyond human control.
2. **The Savior promised a Comforter** — Before His Crucifixion, Jesus promised His disciples the Holy Ghost, who would abide with them.
3. **Peace does not require perfect circumstances** — Elder Eyring recounts giving a priesthood blessing to a father grieving the death of his unborn child. The promised blessing was peace, not the reversal of the loss.
4. **The Resurrection gives us eternal hope** — Jesus Christ overcame physical death through His Resurrection. Through His Atonement, He also made it possible for us to overcome spiritual separation from God.
5. **We are called to comfort one another** — Our baptismal covenant includes sharing one another's burdens, mourning together, and offering comfort.
6. **Seek the Holy Ghost's constant companionship** — The Holy Ghost can guide us toward Jesus Christ as we pray, repent, keep our covenants, worship, and respond to spiritual impressions.

### Seven-day personal invitation

**My seven-day practice**

| Day | Practice |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Identify one burden beyond your control. Bring it to Heavenly Father in prayer. |
| Day 2 | Read Alma 7:11–12. Reflect on how completely the Savior understands you. |
| Day 3 | Read John 14:16–18. Make quiet time to listen. |
| Day 4 | Read Philippians 4:6–7. Pray for peace and your next faithful step. |
| Day 5 | Read 2 Nephi 9:10–12. Reflect on the hope of the Resurrection. |
| Day 6 | Comfort one person through listening or a specific act of service. |
| Day 7 | Reflect on what you learned and give thanks for the Lord's help. |
