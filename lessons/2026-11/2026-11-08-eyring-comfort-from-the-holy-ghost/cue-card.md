## DOCUMENT 2 — ONE-PAGE FACILITATOR CUE CARD

### Chunk 1 of 1

ELDERS QUORUM — SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 2026

COMFORT FROM THE HOLY GHOST
Elder Matthew J. Eyring | October 2026 General Conference

30-MINUTE DISCUSSION | FACILITATOR QUICK REFERENCE

Central message: The Holy Ghost brings the Savior's peace during hardships we cannot resolve alone. Receiving that comfort helps us become instruments of comfort for others.

0–2 min

OPEN — “I can’t fix this.”

Introduce the grieving father in Elder Eyring's talk.

Ask: Have you ever faced something you simply could not fix?

Establish a respectful, voluntary-sharing environment.

2–5 min

1. THE LIMITS OF SELF-RELIANCE

Read: Alma 7:11–12.

Ask: What can faithful people do when their best efforts cannot solve a problem?

Principle: Christ understands our suffering perfectly.

5–8 min

2. WHAT COMFORT MEANS

Ask: Does comfort always mean the problem disappears?

Principle: Peace and unresolved difficulties can coexist.

8–12 min

3. THE PROMISED COMFORTER

Read: John 14:16–17.

Ask: Why did Jesus promise another Comforter?

Principle: The Holy Ghost brings truth, guidance, and peace. Help may come through people and practical resources.

12–16 min

4. PEACE BEYOND UNDERSTANDING

Tell: Elder Eyring's experience with the grieving couple and priesthood blessing.

Read: Philippians 4:6–7.

Ask: How is Christ's peace different from having our circumstances improve?

16–20 min

5. THE HOPE OF RESURRECTION

Read: 2 Nephi 9:10–12.

Ask: How does the Resurrection change our understanding of suffering and death?

Principle: Christ overcomes physical and spiritual death.

20–24 min

6. BECOMING A COMFORTER

Read: Mosiah 18:8–10.

Ask: How do we mourn with others without trying to fix everything?

Principle: Listen, empathize, offer specific help, minister.

24–27 min

7. CONSTANT COMPANIONSHIP

Read: Doctrine and Covenants 88:63.

Ask: What daily choices make us more receptive to the Holy Ghost?

Principle: Pray, repent, worship, listen, and act.

27–29 min

SEVEN-DAY INVITATION

Identify one burden → pray daily → seek quiet spiritual time → comfort one person → reflect.

29–30 min

CLOSE

Read: John 14:18.

Bear testimony of Christ, His Resurrection, and the Holy Ghost's comforting influence.

Facilitation guardrails: Invite, never pressure, personal disclosures. Do not equate suffering with unworthiness. Avoid unsolicited explanations for grief. Welcome professional and practical assistance. Protect the final three minutes for commitment and testimony.

If discussion runs long: Prioritize John 14:16–17, Philippians 4:6–7, Mosiah 18:8–10, and the seven-day invitation.
