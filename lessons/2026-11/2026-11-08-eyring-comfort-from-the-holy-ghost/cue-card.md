# ELDERS QUORUM — FACILITATOR CUE CARD

**Sunday, November 8, 2026 | 30 Minutes**

**Talk:** *Comfort from the Holy Ghost*  
**Speaker:** Elder Matthew J. Eyring  
**Conference:** October 2026 General Conference

[Official Conference Talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2026/10/29eyring?lang=eng)

**CENTRAL MESSAGE**

> The Holy Ghost brings the Savior's peace during hardships we cannot resolve alone. Receiving that comfort helps us become instruments of comfort for others.

---

## 0–2 min | Open — “I can't fix this.”

**SAY:** Introduce the grieving father in Elder Eyring's talk.

**ASK:** Have you ever faced something you simply could not fix?

**SAY:** Establish a respectful, voluntary-sharing environment.

---

## 2–5 min | 1. The limits of self-reliance

**READ:** [Alma 7:11–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/7?id=p11-p12&lang=eng)

**ASK:** What can faithful people do when their best efforts cannot solve a problem?

**PRINCIPLE:** Christ understands our suffering perfectly.

---

## 5–8 min | 2. What comfort means

**ASK:** Does comfort always mean the problem disappears?

**PRINCIPLE:** Peace and unresolved difficulties can coexist.

---

## 8–12 min | 3. The promised Comforter

**READ:** [John 14:16–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?id=p16-p17&lang=eng)

**ASK:** Why did Jesus promise another Comforter?

**PRINCIPLE:** The Holy Ghost brings truth, guidance, and peace. Help may come through people and practical resources.

---

## 12–16 min | 4. Peace beyond understanding

**SAY:** Tell of Elder Eyring's experience with the grieving couple and the priesthood blessing.

**READ:** [Philippians 4:6–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/phil/4?id=p6-p7&lang=eng)

**ASK:** How is Christ's peace different from having our circumstances improve?

---

## 16–20 min | 5. The hope of resurrection

**READ:** [2 Nephi 9:10–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/9?id=p10-p12&lang=eng)

**ASK:** How does the Resurrection change our understanding of suffering and death?

**PRINCIPLE:** Christ overcomes physical and spiritual death.

---

## 20–24 min | 6. Becoming a comforter

**READ:** [Mosiah 18:8–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/18?id=p8-p10&lang=eng)

**ASK:** How do we mourn with others without trying to fix everything?

**PRINCIPLE:** Listen, empathize, offer specific help, minister.

---

## 24–27 min | 7. Constant companionship

**READ:** [Doctrine and Covenants 88:63](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/88?id=p63&lang=eng)

**ASK:** What daily choices make us more receptive to the Holy Ghost?

**PRINCIPLE:** Pray, repent, worship, listen, and act.

---

## 27–29 min | Seven-day invitation

**SAY:** Identify one burden → pray daily → seek quiet spiritual time → comfort one person → reflect.

---

## 29–30 min | Close

**READ:** [John 14:18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?id=p18&lang=eng)

**CLOSING:** Bear testimony of Christ, His Resurrection, and the Holy Ghost's comforting influence.

---

## Exact speaker quote

**SPEAKER QUOTE:** “I can't fix this.”

**SOURCE:** Elder Eyring, *Comfort from the Holy Ghost* (the father's thought, as recorded in the talk).

---

## Facilitation guardrails

Invite, never pressure, personal disclosures. Do not equate suffering with unworthiness. Avoid unsolicited explanations for grief. Welcome professional and practical assistance. Protect the final three minutes for commitment and testimony.

**Time discipline:** If discussion runs long, prioritize John 14:16–17, Philippians 4:6–7, Mosiah 18:8–10, and the seven-day invitation.
